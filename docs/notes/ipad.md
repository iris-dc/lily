# iPad and regular widths

> `LayoutMode`, the split Chats view, grids, readable columns, form sheets, the wide landing. Moved out of `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-17 and kept as written. The reference part describes the feature as the README did; the dated notes record decisions, lessons and the evidence behind them, newest last, so an older sentence can be superseded by a later one or by a marker such as "(removed 2026-10-14)". Read this file before touching the area; the rules that apply everywhere are in `AGENTS.md`.

## Reference (from the README)

- The deployment target is **iOS 26.0** (since 2026-10-07; 26.5 before), so every iPhone from the iPhone 11 and the iPhone SE (2020) and every iPad on iPadOS 26 can install it. The iPhone is portrait-only; the iPad rotates freely and supports Split View, Slide Over and Stage Manager, because every layout follows the window's size class (`LayoutMode`, see `AGENTS.md`, UI and design rules, and the P0 note below), never the device. On an iPad the tabs sit in the top bar, Explore, Home and Discover lay their cards out in as many columns as fit, details and forms keep a readable column, sheets that are forms open as centred form sheets, the landing shows its miniature beside the caption, a wide Explore keeps the map beside the content instead of behind a switch, the Chats tab is a split view (the conversations on the left, the open room or the inbox on the right), a hardware keyboard's Return sends a message (Shift+Return starts a new line), and ⌘1 to ⌘4 switch tabs, ⌘N starts a game, ⌘⇧N a group and ⌘⇧T a tournament (the iPadOS menu bar lists them).

`LilyIPadTests` checks the regular-width layouts (Explore's group grid, the card grid and the map pane beside it, Discover's grid, the Chats split view keeping the list beside the room through a rotation, the inbox opening in the detail column, a create sheet presented as a centred form sheet, the landing's miniature beside its caption) and skips itself on a phone, where the `ui` stage runs every class.

## Dated notes

- **iPad (2026-10-07, plan `~/.claude/projects/-Users-makshr-lily/groups-plan/ipad-plan.md`; slice P0, the foundations).** The app is iOS 26+ only and gets a native iPad layout; the deployment target dropped from 26.5 to **26.0** (a 26.0 build proved nothing newer is used), the iPhone is **portrait-only** (`INFOPLIST_KEY_UISupportedInterfaceOrientations_iPhone = UIInterfaceOrientationPortrait`; landscape on the phone added nothing and the landing lost its miniature in it), the iPad keeps all four orientations and multitasking (no `UIRequiresFullScreen`). **Every adaptive view switches on the window's size class, never on the device**: `LayoutMode` (`Core/UI/Layout/LayoutMode.swift`; `compact` = every iPhone and a narrow iPad window, `regular` = an iPad window of regular width, `wide` = regular and at least `DesignTokens.Layout.wideMinWidth` 1000 pt, for Explore's two panes) is resolved from `horizontalSizeClass` and the width `layoutModeProvider()` measures with `onGeometryChange` on `AppRootView`'s root `Group`, read as `@Environment(\.layoutMode)`; a Split View third of an iPad is compact and gets the phone layout live. **Compact must stay pixel-identical to the iPhone screens of today**, which the existing UI tests prove. Shared layout pieces live in `Core/UI/Layout/`: `readableColumn(maxWidth:)` (a centred column capped at `Layout.readableWidth` 720; a no-op under that width), `AdaptiveCardGrid` (a `LazyVGrid` with one flexible column on compact, so it draws exactly what a `LazyVStack` at `Spacing.md` does, and `GridItem(.adaptive(minimum: cardMinWidth 340, maximum: cardMaxWidth 520))` on regular; `columns(for:)` is the tested rule) and `lilyHoverable()` (`.hoverEffect(.highlight)` for a pointer); the tokens are in `DesignTokens+Layout.swift` and the identifiers (`explore-map-pane`, `chats-sidebar`) in `AccessibilityIdentifiers+Layout.swift`, both `nonisolated extension`s. P0 wires none of them into a screen yet (P1 to P3 do); `LandingHeadline` gained `lineLimit(1)` + `minimumScaleFactor(Layout.headlineMinimumScale 0.8)` per line, because on the iPhone SE's 375 pt "Real games near you." truncated to "Real games nea…". CI: `ci.sh ui-ipad` runs `lilyUITests/LilyIPadTests` alone on an iPad (`pick_ipad` (`pick_device iPad` since 2026-10-14) prefers "iPad Pro 13-inch", else the first available iPad; `XCODEBUILD_DESTINATION` is how `run_xcodebuild` is pointed at it), and `all` runs that class `test-without-building` from the iPhone's build-for-testing products after the iPhone UI stage (both simulators are arm64, so no second build). `GridItem.Size` is not `Equatable`: pattern-match it in tests. Tests: `LayoutModeTests`, `AdaptiveCardGridTests` (`lilyTests/UI/LayoutModeTests.swift`).
- **iPad slice P1 (2026-10-07): the shell and Chats on a regular width.** `MainTabView` keeps the default tab style: iPadOS draws the four tabs in a top bar on its own, the iPhone keeps its floating bar (`.sidebarAdaptable` was tried in P1 and dropped after P2's screenshots: its sidebar only repeated the four tabs and iPadOS opened it by itself in landscape, floating over 280 pt of the content; the plan's "Your groups" sidebar section stays out of scope), and XCUITest sees the iPad's tabs as plain `Button`s nested in another with the same label and **no `tabBar` element**, so `LilyUITestCase.openTab(titled:)` falls back to the first button with that exact label when `app.tabBars.buttons[title]` is absent (`app.buttons["Chats"]` alone fails with "Multiple matching elements"). `AppNavigation` is still ignorant of size classes: `usesSplitChats` is set by `MainTabView` from `layoutMode.isRegular` (`onChange(initial: true)`), and with it `open(chat:)` and the new `openInbox()` **select** (`selectedConversation: ConversationSelection?`, `.inbox` / `.room(id:)`, after `popChatToRoot()`, so a screen pushed over the previous room never hides the new one) instead of pushing; `sessionDidEnd` clears the selection and `pendingCreate`. `ChatTab` draws `ChatsSplitView` for a signed-in caller on a regular width (a guest keeps the full-width sign-in prompt in the compact stack): a `NavigationSplitView(columnVisibility: .constant(.all))`, `.balanced`, whose sidebar is `ConversationList` (shared with the compact `ChatsView`; the inbox row and `GroupRowList` are buttons now, `InboxRow(inbox:isSelected:onOpen:)`, `GroupRowList(selectedID:)`, the selected row washed by `selectedRowBackground(_:)` at `Opacity.selectedRow`) under `.accessibilityElement(children: .contain)` + `chats-sidebar`, and whose detail is the `NavigationStack(path: chatPath)` rooted on `ConversationDetail` (the inbox, the room `.id(id)` so appear/cancel run per room, or `chats-nothing-chosen` "Pick a conversation"), registering `conversationDestinations(dependencies:)` (the group screens, `InboxDestination`, the stack's `SportEvent`) exactly like the compact root; `conversationLoads(dependencies:)` is the shared `.task(id:)`. `ChatView` takes `onGone:` (the split view clears the selection; the pushed room still pops) and, like `InboxView`, hides the tab bar on `.compact` only. **Rows must not size themselves with `containerRelativeFrame`**: iPadOS 26 floats the sidebar over the detail and reports it as a horizontal safe-area inset, so the scroll view's container runs under the sidebar and 78 % of it overflowed the visible column on both sides (bubbles cut at the sidebar's edge and at the screen's, seen 2026-10-07); `ChatTranscript` now measures its usable width (`size.width` minus the horizontal `safeAreaInsets`, `measuringTranscriptWidth`) into `\.transcriptWidth` and `bubbleRow`/`galleryWidth` size through `transcriptFraction(_:alignment:)`, which falls back to `containerRelativeFrame` until measured (one frame). `MessageComposer` handles a hardware Return through `ReturnKeyPolicy` (`onKeyPress(keys: [.return])`, the only overload whose action receives the `KeyPress`): plain Return sends while `canSend`, is swallowed otherwise, Shift+Return is `.ignored` so the field inserts its newline; the software keyboard never reaches `onKeyPress`. `LilyApp` registers `ShellCommands` (`.commands`): ⌘1–⌘4 over `AppTab.shown` (the drawn tabs, so the numbers never skip when chat is off; `AppTab.title`), ⌘N/⌘⇧N/⌘⇧T call `navigation.requestCreate(_:)` with a `CreateIntent` (`Features/Shell/Models`) that Explore's `EventListView` consumes in `.onChange(of: navigation.pendingCreate, initial: true)` (`creatable` lists only; a guest gets sign-in as from the "+"). `LilyIPadTests` skips itself unless `UIDevice.current.userInterfaceIdiom == .pad`, because the iPhone `ui` stage runs every class. - **iPad slice P2 (2026-10-07): grids on Explore, Home and Discover, and the wide Explore's map pane.** `EventCardList`, `TournamentCardList` and `DiscoverGroupList` draw through `AdaptiveCardGrid` (one column on compact, exactly the old `LazyVStack`; `GridItem(.adaptive(minimum: cardMinWidth 340, maximum: cardMaxWidth 520), alignment: .top)` on regular, so two cards of different heights start on one line); `GroupCarousel` and `TournamentCarousel` keep their sideways rows on compact and draw `TileGrid` (`Core/UI/Layout`: a wrapping `LazyVGrid` at `tileMinWidth` 250, the first `regularTileCount` 8 items, `shown(of:)` is the tested cap) on regular, through `GroupTileGrid` (in `GroupCarousel.swift`, shared with Home's groups section, whose rows stay on compact) and tiles that take `fillsWidth: true` (a fixed 250 pt in the carousel, the cell's width in the grid). **A `TileGrid` carrying the carousel's identifier needs `.accessibilityElement(children: .contain)` first**, or `group-carousel` is stamped on every tile and no `group-row-<id>` exists (the container-identifier lesson again); and it is no scroll view, so a test finds it as `app.descendants(matching: .any)["group-carousel"]`. `EventListView` reads `\.layoutMode` into `EventListLayout.resolve(mode:showsMap:)` (`Features/Events/Models`, tested): `.sideBySide` on `.wide` with a map = `HStack` of the content column (`containerRelativeFrame(.horizontal)` at `exploreContentFraction` 0.55, the "+" over its corner) and `EventsMapView` as `explore-map-pane` (`.accessibilityElement(children: .contain)` before the identifier), the list/map picker gone and the title large; `.switchable` everywhere else keeps the picker. - **iPad slice P4 (2026-10-08): review and screenshot pass; completes the plan.** Four fixes came out of it, all worth knowing. (1) **Safe-area insets are anchored to the window, not to the view**: the Chats detail column sits *beside* the floating sidebar's leading inset, so `width - leading - trailing` (P1's transcript measure) squeezed every iPad bubble to a third of the column (124 pt of 434 on the iPad mini); `UsableWidth.of(leading:trailing:frameInWindow:windowWidth:)` (the `width:` argument went 2026-10-14; it was always `frameInWindow.width`) (`Core/UI/Layout/UsableWidth.swift`, `UsableWidthTests`) subtracts only the part of each inset that overlaps the view's frame in the window (`proxy.frame(in: .global)`), the trailing one judged against `\.windowWidth`, which `layoutModeProvider()` publishes from the root's measured width. A named coordinate space on the root (`bounds(of:)`) was the first cut and answered `nil` inside the split view's detail column, which is hosted apart from the root, so never rely on one across a `NavigationSplitView` or `TabView` boundary. `measuringTranscriptWidth` feeds it and `transcriptFraction(_:alignment:)` falls back to `containerRelativeFrame` until the first measure. (2) `lilyFormSheet()` (`Core/UI/Layout/FormSheet.swift`) is the one `presentationSizing(.form)`; every form sheet (create/edit game, group, tournament, team name, sign-in, invite, report, match) uses it; compact ignores it. (3) The landing headline is drawn by `HeadlineLine`, a `ViewThatFits` over one line at full size, one line at `headlineMinimumScale` (`LilyTheme.Fonts.headline(scale:)`), then wrapped: a plain `lineLimit(1)` + `minimumScaleFactor` fixed the SE in English but cut the French and Spanish lines to "De vrais matchs près de…" on every phone; the headline stack is `fixedSize(horizontal: false, vertical: true)` so a short page shrinks the miniature, never the words. (4) `TournamentDetailViewModel` adds a room the caller just entered to Mine (`myGroups.add`) like an accepted invite does, because the Chats split view lists only what Mine holds and Mine's by-user index may lag a moment. Tiles take `tileFrame(fixedWidth:height:fillsWidth:)`: fixed in the carousels, the cell's width in the regular-width `TileGrid`, which `TournamentCarousel` draws too. `.sidebarAdaptable` is gone (plan Deviations): iPadOS opened its sidebar by itself in landscape and it only repeated the four tabs. Screenshots of landing, Explore, event detail, Home, Chats, group detail, create sheet and Profile on the iPad Pro 13-inch (both orientations), iPad mini, iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone SE were judged (`/tmp/ipad-p4/shots/`); the French landing was captured too.
