## Reference (from the README)

- Tournaments (debug builds, behind `AppConfig.FeatureFlags.tournaments`, since 2026-10-04): organiser-run competitions for one event type with a format (single elimination or round robin), entries (players alone, or teams a captain names and teammates join), a chat room of their own, and once the organiser starts it a bracket or standings, matches whose results the players report and confirm (the organiser records, decides disputes and gives walkovers), and system rows in the room for the start, every result, every dispute and the winner. A "Tournaments" carousel sits under the groups on Explore with "See all" leading to Discover tournaments (soonest first, a type dropdown, distances), Home shows "Your tournaments" between your groups and your games, a group's detail has a Tournaments segment and "Create tournament" in its menu, the "+" on Explore offers "New tournament", and the Chats tab lists every tournament you organise or play in as a room captioned "Tournament · N players" with a trophy for its mark. Entrants and the organiser invite people from the detail's menu (the invite lands in the inbox as a card that names the tournament's shape; Accept enters you, or opens the detail to pick a team), the organiser sets a time and a place for any match from its sheet (a match reminder reaches both sides an hour before, in the inbox and as a push whose tap opens the match), anyone may report a tournament, and a finished one shows its winner with the trophy. See [Tournaments](#tournaments).

### Tournaments

A tournament (slice L1 of the tournaments plan, 2026-10-04; debug builds, behind `AppConfig.FeatureFlags.tournaments`) is an organiser-run competition for one event type: a `format` (`single_elimination`, byes to the next power of two with seed 1 meeting seed N, or `round_robin`, the circle method), `teamSize` (1 means players enter alone; more means a captain names a team and teammates join it until it is full; one entry per user), `maxEntries` (2 to 64 for a bracket, 3 to 12 for a round robin: a start writes every match in one transaction; `AppConfig.Tournaments.entriesRange(for:)` runs from `minEntries(for:)` to `maxEntries(for:)`, the form's stepper takes that floor through `TournamentDraft.Rules.entriesRange(for:)`, `TournamentDraft.select(format:)` raises two entries to three when the format moves to a round robin and lowers nothing on the way back, and a round robin stored at 2 shows "Between 3 and 12 entries" when edited until it is raised; Laurel refuses it too, as a bare `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` with no code, so the mock answers each route's fallback), `allowsDraws` (team sports by default, racket sports, esports and board games not; always sent by the app), a start, an optional registration deadline before it, a place, and optionally the group that hosts it (then its visibility is the group's and creating it follows "members can create games"); a standalone one picks public or private. The lifecycle is `registration` -> `in_progress` (the organiser starts it by hand from the detail's menu once the format's minimum is in: 2 entries for a bracket, 3 for a round robin) -> `completed` (the final's result in a bracket, the last result in a round robin) or `cancelled`; entries are taken while in registration and before the deadline.

- **The room is a group.** A tournament's chat is a `Group` of kind `tournament` under the tournament's id: the organiser holds the `owner` row, every player a `member` row; `name`, `visibility` and `type` are the tournament's, `maxMembers` is `maxEntries * teamSize + 1`, both member permissions are off. `GET /api/groups?scope=mine` lists it, so the Chats tab shows it like any room (trophy mark, "Tournament · 6 players"); it is never on Discover groups, never a community on Home or in the forms (`SportGroup.isCommunity`), and `GroupAccess.room` gives its members chat and the roster only: entering and leaving go through the tournament's entries, and the group routes (join, leave, edit, delete, invites, roles) answer `403` for it. A room row on Chats opens the chat, whose info button opens the tournament; a room pushed as a group (a profile's "Groups in common") opens the tournament too (`GroupDestinations`).
- **Reads and writes.** `GET /api/tournaments?scope=upcoming[&type=&lat=&lon=]` (public, in registration, soonest first; the coarse position is for the statistics only), `?scope=mine` (every tournament whose room the caller is in: in progress first, then registration by start, then the finished newest first), `GET /api/groups/{id}/tournaments` (all but cancelled), `GET /api/tournaments/{id}` answering `TournamentDetail {tournament, entries, matches, standings}` (anyone for a public one; a private one to its players, its organiser and the hosting group's members, `404 TOURNAMENT_NOT_FOUND` otherwise). `POST /api/tournaments` with `CreateTournamentPayload` (`clientTournamentId` chosen once per draft, so a create repeated after a lost answer finds its tournament; `visibility` omitted with a `groupId`), `PUT /api/tournaments/{id}` with `UpdateTournamentPayload` (every field while in registration; after the start only the name, description and place may differ, `409 TOURNAMENT_LOCKED` otherwise, and `maxEntries` never drops below the entries in: `409 CAPACITY_TOO_LOW`), `POST .../entries` (`{name}` for a team, empty for an individual) answering the `Entry`, `POST .../entries/{entryId}/members` (join a team), `DELETE .../entries/{entryId}/members/{userId}` (leave, or the captain or organiser removes a player;
