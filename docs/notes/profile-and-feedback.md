# Profile and the contact form

> The contact form on Profile; the Language row is in `localization.md`, the Currency row in `events.md`. Moved out of `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-17 and kept as written. The reference part describes the feature as the README did; the dated notes record decisions, lessons and the evidence behind them, newest last, so an older sentence can be superseded by a later one or by a marker such as "(removed 2026-10-14)". Read this file before touching the area; the rules that apply everywhere are in `AGENTS.md`.

## Reference (from the README)

- Contact form (2026-10-08): a "Contact & feedback" row on Profile opens a sheet with what the message is about (Question, Feedback, Bug), the message, a reply-to email prefilled from the account, and a footer naming what is attached (app version, iOS version, device model). It posts to `POST /api/feedback`; the backend keeps the item for operators and mails the owner. A guest is sent to sign in first; a suspended account may still write (an appeal has to reach us). `-mock-events` accepts the send in memory.

- **The Language and Currency cards take a tap anywhere (2026-10-26, on the user's "let me press Language or Currency anywhere on the button, not only on System").** The cards held the menu in a `Picker(.menu)` (language) or a `Menu` (currency) at the trailing edge, so only the value was the button. `SettingsMenuRow` (`Features/Profile/Views`) first made the whole `GlassCard` the menu's label, which brought the blink the user reported the same day ("the language selection button still blinks"): iOS hides a menu's label while the menu is up and morphs it back on dismissal, so the card vanished for four frames and grew back over six at every open and close (seen frame by frame once the Profile tree was no longer rebuilt, `localization.md`). Now the card is plain content (icon, title, value, accent chevrons) under a `Menu` whose label is a `Color.clear` of the card's size: the tap lands anywhere on the card, the menu grows from an empty snapshot, and nothing on the card disappears. The card is hidden from accessibility and the menu speaks for it (`accessibilityLabel` "<title>, <value>", the row's identifier on the menu), so `app.buttons["profile-currency"].label` still reads the title and the choice. The `Picker` stays the menu's items for their checkmarks; `LanguageRow` and `CurrencyRow` draw their value and items through it. Recorded again: the card is at full size in every frame of the menu's opening and of two picks, the menu grows out of the card's centre and shrinks away in about five frames, and the card's text crossfades in place; the card shows no press highlight now (the transparent label has nothing to dim), and the open menu rests over the card's middle, as a menu anchored to its centre does. Checked on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator: a tap 8 % into the Language card and one at 40 % of the Currency card open the menus; the cards' glass, edges and colours measured identical before and after; `LilyLanguageTests` and `LilyProfileTests` pass unchanged. Seen in passing, not changed: "NOK · Norwegian Krone" wraps to two lines in the currency menu, and the Contact card's glass is lighter than the two menu cards'.
