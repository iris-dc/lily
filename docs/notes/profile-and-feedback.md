# Profile and the contact form

> The contact form on Profile; the Language row is in `localization.md`, the Currency row in `events.md`. Moved out of `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-17 and kept as written. The reference part describes the feature as the README did; the dated notes record decisions, lessons and the evidence behind them, newest last, so an older sentence can be superseded by a later one or by a marker such as "(removed 2026-10-14)". Read this file before touching the area; the rules that apply everywhere are in `AGENTS.md`.

## Reference (from the README)

- Contact form (2026-10-08): a "Contact & feedback" row on Profile opens a sheet with what the message is about (Question, Feedback, Bug), the message, a reply-to email prefilled from the account, and a footer naming what is attached (app version, iOS version, device model). It posts to `POST /api/feedback`; the backend keeps the item for operators and mails the owner. A guest is sent to sign in first; a suspended account may still write (an appeal has to reach us). `-mock-events` accepts the send in memory.

## Dated notes

- **Contact form (2026-10-08; contract in `/tmp/feedback/contract.md` at the time, mirrored in Laurel's `FeedbackRequest` and README).** `Features/Feedback/{Models,Interfaces,Implementations,ViewModels,Views}`: `FeedbackKind` (`contact | feedback | bug`, the wire names; - **The Language and Currency cards take a tap anywhere (2026-10-26, on the user's "let me press Language or Currency anywhere on the button, not only on System"; the `Menu` this describes was superseded the same day by the popover entry below).** The cards held the menu in a `Picker(.menu)` (language) or a `Menu` (currency) at the trailing edge, so only the value was the button. `SettingsMenuRow` (`Features/Profile/Views`) now makes the whole `GlassCard` the menu's label (icon, title, the value in white, the chevrons in the accent; `.menuStyle(.button)` and `.buttonStyle(.plain)` so the label keeps its colours), with the `Picker` as the menu's items for their checkmarks; `LanguageRow` and `CurrencyRow` draw their value and items through it. Checked on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator: a tap 8 % into the Language card and one at 40 % of the Currency card open the menus; the cards' glass, edges and colours measured identical before and after; `LilyLanguageTests` and `LilyProfileTests` pass unchanged. Seen in passing, not changed: "NOK · Norwegian Krone" wraps to two lines in the currency menu, and the Contact card's glass is lighter than the two menu cards'.
- **The settings cards drop their choices down as a popover (2026-10-26, later, on the user's "after I change a language and press the Language button again, the button jumps and blinks a bit", then "no, revert it, now it blinks all the time differently" to a first attempt).** Measured on recordings of the whole-card `Menu` (the state committed as 605a54b): every opening, before and after a pick, was identical; the jump and blink lived in every close without a pick, in every language, and were iOS morphing the menu's label into the menu and back: the card's slot empty for 8 to 11 frames, the card zooming back from about half size with a 3 to 4 pt overshoot, its text re-rasterising, then one frame redrawing the whole card about 14 frames later (the snapshot handing over to the live view). Two attempts kept the `Menu`: a transparent label of the card's size over a plain card read clean on the simulator but "blinks all the time differently" on the phone (reverted, e6c07b0 → 7047260), and the glass kept out of the label showed why: a `GlassCard` under a presented menu loses its material and comes back black until a later redraw (0.7 s once, 4.6 s another time), so on the phone the card under the menu was flashing. So `SettingsChoiceRow` (`Features/Profile/Views`, replacing `SettingsMenuRow`) is a plain `Button` whose label is the glass card, opening a `.popover` kept a popover on iPhone (`presentationCompactAdaptation(.popover)`, as the Explore filter's `FilterDropdownButton`) with the choices (`SettingsChoice<Value>`: value and title) as 44 pt rows on clear glass with `Symbols.chosen` on the current one; a pick or a tap outside closes it. The card is never a menu label, so nothing morphs: on the recording its rims sat on the same pixel rows through 306 frames of opening and closing, with a press highlight and the popover growing from its arrow over about 230 ms. A first cut used the filter's `DropdownPanel` (title and Done) opening downward: nine language rows under a 64 pt title row overflowed the cap, cut a row mid-glyph and lay over the floating tab bar; the list is now rows only, opens upward (`arrowEdge: .bottom`, where Profile has room; it covers the large title but the first letter, as a popover does), takes the card's width up to `dropdownMaxWidth` (measured with `onGeometryChange`; a first cut at the filter's 340 pt sat 15 pt inside the card on each side) and it shows `dropdownRows` (six) whole rows, scaled with the type, with no inner padding, so it ends on a row's edge before it scrolls (with the padding a 4 pt sliver of the seventh row read as a double bottom line); the tokens live in `DesignTokens+Settings.swift`. The pick no longer runs inside `withAnimation`: with Profile relabelling in place, the animation slid the cards' new values into their chevrons over ten frames. `LanguageRow` and `CurrencyRow` hand it their choices and the current value; `LilyLanguageTests` and `LilyProfileTests` pass unchanged (the rows are buttons named by their titles, as the menu items were).
