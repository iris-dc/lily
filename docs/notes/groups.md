## Reference (from the README)

- Groups (debug builds, behind `AppConfig.FeatureFlags.groups`): your groups on Home (a conversation list: initials, name with a lock or globe, "34 members · Football · Active today"; a row opens the group's detail) above your games; groups are founded from the "+" on Explore (New group opens the create form: name, description, event type, public or private, what members may do); on Explore a "Groups" carousel above the list (then an "Events" heading over the cards), two rows of tiles that scroll sideways, whose "See all" pushes Discover (public groups as cards, a search field that is always shown, an event-type dropdown, paging); and a group detail with the actions your role allows (Open chat, Invite, Create game, Join group, "Invite only", "Group is full"), Events, Tournaments and Members segments as the caller's access allows (Tournaments for anyone on a public group, guests included, and for the members of a private one; Members for members and for every signed-in caller of a public group; the picker shows once there is more than one), the roster with role badges, a context menu with Make admin / Remove / Ban only on the rows the caller may act on (a row without actions has no menu; a ban re-reads the group for its count), a Banned list with Unban, and a toolbar menu with Invite, Edit, Leave, Delete and, for members, admins and signed-in readers of a public group (never the owner or a guest), Report group, which opens the report sheet. Inviting is direct: the Invite sheet lists the people you share a group or a game with ("In Kreuzberg Kickers", "Played Sunset 5-a-side"), a name search narrows them, and one tap sends the invite, which the invitee answers from the inbox on their Chats tab (see [Chats and inbox](inbox-people-and-push.md#chats-and-inbox)); no codes, no links. A room on the Chats tab opens the group's chat: bubbles in the accent for your own messages and on the surface colour for everyone else's, grouped into runs with the sender's name and avatar on the first and the time under the last, day chips, a centred "Marta created Sunday 5-a-side" row that opens the game, a composer that grows to five lines with a counter near the limit, your message shown at once and marked "Not sent. Tap to retry." when it fails (Retry / Delete in its menu; a failed or still-sending bubble survives leaving the room and is there on the next open, until a sign-out), long-press for Reply, Copy and Delete, and on another member's message Report and Block (a block hides their rows at once), older pages loading as you scroll up without the list jumping, a first page that fails showing "Couldn't load messages" with Try again, an unread dot on the row and a badge on the Chats tab until you open the room, an info button that leads to the group, and a "more" menu whose "Clear chat" empties the room for you alone after a confirmation (other members keep their copy; a long press on the room's Chats row offers the same). Games live in groups too: the event form has a Group row (a menu of the groups you may create games in, preset and read-only when the form opens from a group's "Create game"), a group's Events segment lists its upcoming games, cards on Explore and in that segment carry the group as a badge, preview cards show it under the caption, and the event detail says "Hosted in <group>" (a link to the group when it is public; "Members only" under it when it is private).

### Groups

Groups are communities around a sport (or "Any type"): a chat room, a member list and the games hosted in the group. The feature ships in debug builds only until it is finished, behind `AppConfig.FeatureFlags.groups` (`Config/AppConfig+FeatureFlags.swift`; `FeatureFlags.chat` gates the Chats tab and the chat screens, and with `groups` on and `chat` off a group row opens the group instead of its chat). A release build has three tabs.

- **Visibility.** A group is `public` (anyone finds it on Discover and joins with one tap) or `private` (reachable only through an invite; a non-member gets "group not found", never its existence). Visibility is chosen at creation and never changes.
- **Where it plays (2026-10-07).** A group may carry a place (`location`, the events' `{name, coordinate}` shape): the form's "Where" section asks for a name and the spot (proposed from the device's position, else set on the map), **required for a public group** (people nearby find it by where it plays), optional for a private one; an edit may change or clear it, and a public group from before places existed asks for one the first time it is edited. Discover without a search sends the user's position (`lat`/`lon`, two decimals, like Explore) and shows what the backend ranks around it, one page without a cursor; a name search is global, as it was. Tiles, cards and the detail show the place and the distance to it; the Explore carousel and the tournaments carousel ask for the position on appearance and load again once it arrives or moves, since the backend ranks both by it.
- **Roles.** The creator is the `owner` (exactly one; cannot leave, may delete the group and change roles), `admin`s edit the group, invite, create games, remove and ban members, and `member`s chat and join games. Per group, two switches say what members may do beyond that: "Create games" (`membersCanCreateEvents`) and "Invite people" (`membersCanInvite`), both on by default. A ban is a marker row, not a membership: a banned user cannot rejoin or be invited until an admin unbans them. `GroupAccess` (`Features/Groups/Models`) turns a group and the caller's id into what the screen may offer, the way `Participation` does for events. Since 2026-09-29 a **public** group's roster is readable by every signed-in user (`GroupAccess.canSeeMembers(in:)`; the Members segment shows, rows open profiles, no actions, no banned list), a private group's stays members-only.
- **Direct invites.** A member with invite rights (owners and admins always, members when "Invite people" is on) opens the Invite sheet from the group's detail and picks a person from **the people they share a group or a game with**: `GET /api/groups/{id}/invitees` answers them sorted by name, each with the first shared group or game as context ("In Kreuzberg Kickers", "Played Sunset 5-a-side") and `isInvited` while a pending invite into this group exists; the group's members and banned users are never among them. `POST /api/groups/{id}/invites` with `{"userId"}` sends the invite and answers it (`SentInvite`, whose id is the invitee's inbox item id); inviting someone who already holds a pending invite answers that invite again. The invitee finds it in the inbox on their Chats tab and taps Accept or Decline (see [Chats and inbox](inbox-people-and-push.md#chats-and-inbox)). Refusals with copy of their own: `ALREADY_MEMBER` ("They're already in this group"), `CANNOT_INVITE` (banned from the group), `USER_NOT_FOUND`, `NOT_A_MEMBER` and `FORBIDDEN`, the per-user rate limit; anything else is "Couldn't send the invite". Invites expire after seven days; there is no code, no link and no name search over every account, so nobody can enumerate users, and revoking a sent invite is a follow-up.
- **Games in groups.** A game may be hosted in a group: the event form's Group row offers the groups the caller may create games in (`MyGroupsStore.eligibleForEvents`) and is preset, read-only, when the form opens from a group's "Create game". A public group's games are on Explore like any other, with the group's name as a badge on the card and "Hosted in <group>" on the detail, which links to the group. A private group's games never reach Explore, on the device too: one you create in a private group shows on Home and under the group, not on the Explore list behind the form, and the mock feed hides the Padel Crew's game the same way. Their detail is served to members and participants only and says "Members only" under the group's name. Every group's upcoming games are listed under the Events segment of its detail.
- **Launch arguments.** `-mock-user-id <sub>` makes every mock sign-in yield that user so two simulators can act as two people (only together with `-mock-auth`; a Cognito user is whoever the pool says). `-mock-terms-pending` (read only together with `-mock-events`) makes the mock account owe the current terms until they are accepted once in the run, so the terms sheet can be seen and tested without a backend; `-mock-chat-replies` makes a fixture member answer every message sent in a mock room after 1.5 s (never passed by the UI tests); `-mock-attachment-picker` makes the composer's attach menu add the bundled fixtures at once instead of opening the system pickers, which run out of process and cannot be driven by XCUITest: "Photo library" adds `MockAttachmentPhoto`, "Camera" (shown without a camera then) the clip `MockAttachmentVideo`, "File" the document `MockAttachmentFile` (the mock run's uploads land in `MockAttachmentStore`, whose `mock://attachments/...` links `MockAttachmentURLProtocol` serves back to the loader); `-realtime-endpoint <url>` names the realtime API for a debug build against a deployed backend (otherwise `GET /api/me` names it, and a local Laurel gets none: chat catches up over REST on appear, after a send and, for the open room, on every return from the background). All are ignored by release builds like every other argument. `-system-push` keeps the system's permission prompt and APNs registration under `-mock-events` (the device registry stays in memory), so the prompt and an `xcrun simctl push <udid> iris.lily payload.json` banner can be seen on a simulator without a backend.
- **Terms.** `GET /api/me` says whether the account has accepted the current terms (`termsAccepted`, `acceptedTermsVersion`), and every content write of an account that has not answers `403 TERMS_REQUIRED`. A signed-in caller who owes them gets the terms sheet (`TermsSheet`, `Features/Moderation`, presented by the root over whatever is on screen) as soon as the account loads or a write is refused that way: "Before you join in", a short message, links to the terms, the privacy policy and support (`AppConfig.Moderation`), and "I agree" (`PUT /api/me/terms` with the version the app re-reads from `GET /api/me` at that moment, so a version bumped while the sheet was open is accepted rather than refused as stale) or "Sign out", the only way past it without agreeing; it cannot be swiped away, scrolls at large type sizes, and closes once the acceptance is recorded or the session ends. Guests are never asked. A refusal on an event write (join, create, edit) shows its popup but raises the sheet only on the next account load, because those view models report to `ErrorCenter` directly (a follow-up).

- **Groups data layer.** `Features/Groups` follows the events layout. `SportGroup` decodes the backend's `Group` directly (named like `SportEvent` because `Group` is a SwiftUI view); `membership` is absent for non-members and banned users, so `role`/`isMember` never read a banned marker as a membership. `GroupAccess(group:userID:)` is the `Participation` analogue (`guest`, `canJoin`, `inviteOnly`, `member(role)`, `full`, `conversation`) with the permission helpers every screen switches on; the server stays the authority. `GroupDraft` validates name (3..60) and description (<= 500) in UTF-16 units against `AppConfig.Groups`, keeps a lower-case UUID `clientId` that the backend uses as the group id (a repeated create answers the same group), and `makeGroup(ownerName:now:)` is the one place a draft becomes a stored group (mock and test fake). Direct invites go through `InviteRepository`: `candidates(groupID:)` reads `GET /api/groups/{id}/invitees` (`{items}`, decoded as `Page<InviteCandidate>`) and `invite(groupID:userID:)` posts `{userId}` to `/api/groups/{id}/invites`, answered by a `SentInvite` whose id is the invitee's inbox item id; `InviteCandidate` carries `via`/`viaName` for the row's caption and `isInvited` for its button. `RemoteGroupRepository` needs the `IdentityProvider` because leaving is `DELETE .../members/<own id>`. Failures map through the shared `BackendErrorCode` table (every code of the backend's error list has a case; `GROUP_ID_TAKEN`/`GROUP_ID_REUSED` become `groupCreationFailed` so the create flow can refetch by client id); the fallbacks are `groupsUnavailable` for reads, `groupActionFailed` for writes, `groupCreationFailed` for creates, `inviteUnavailable` for both invite routes, `inboxUnavailable` for inbox reads and `inviteActionFailed` for accept and decline, `reportFailed` for reports. `MyGroupsStore` (app-lifetime, on `AppDependencies.myGroups`) is the one source of the caller's groups (`reload()`, `loadIfStale()` through `StoreLoader` over `AppConfig.Lists` and `AppDependencies.groupChanges`: a version that moved while the request was out is loaded once more, exactly once, the first answer applied so the screen never empties; `add`/`replace`/`remove`, a `replace` of a group not held inserting nothing; `eligibleForEvents`; `SportGroup.dropping(_:)` is the one "a member left" arithmetic the mocks and the fake share); `MeStore` (`AppDependencies.me`) holds `GET /api/me` (`Account`: operator flag, terms owed, realtime endpoint, attachments enabled) and accepts the terms (reloading the account first and sending the version it just read, so a version bumped meanwhile is accepted rather than refused as stale; `needsTerms` drives the terms sheet, see [Groups](#groups)); both are `SessionObserver`s cleared on sign-out, run their request in a task of their own keyed by user (`SingleFlight.run(key:)`: a call for the same user joins the run in flight, one for another user waits it out and then runs its own, and a caller cancelled mid-load, like the shell's sync on a scene-phase change, does not lose it) and drop an answer that arrives for a previous caller. The mock mirrors the backend's route rules: a direct conversation or a tournament room refuses the group routes (edit, delete, bans, leave, join) with `.insufficientRole`, a member's own join is replayed before the visibility guard, and the host cannot leave their own game. Mocks: six fixture groups (Kreuzberg Kickers as member with an unread room, Tempelhof Runners as admin, Sunday Padel Crew as owner, Berlin Basketball to join, Spree Volley full, Climbing Buddies private and reachable only by accepting the mock inbox's invite from Noor), rosters of a few named members plus the caller as "You", invite candidates computed from those rosters and the mock games' hosts, `MockMeRepository` making the Apple tester an operator (and owing the terms under `-mock-terms-pending` until accepted once), `MockModerationRepository` keeping blocks in memory. `-mock-events` swaps every one of them along with the events ones.
- **Groups screens.** Every `NavigationStack` root (Home, Explore, Chats) applies `.groupDestinations(dependencies:)`, which registers `SportGroup` -> `GroupDetailView(.standalone)`, `GroupInfoDestination` -> the detail reached from a chat (`.fromChat`, "Open chat" hidden), `EventGroupRef` -> `GroupLoaderView` (one fetch, then the detail, which stays through a failed reload and on the way back from a pushed screen; "This group is no longer available" on a 404, a retry on anything else) and `ChatDestination` -> `ChatView`, so an event's "Hosted in" link works on every tab, plus `DiscoverGroupsDestination` -> `DiscoverGroupsView`; each stack registers `SportEvent` once (`HomeView`, `EventListView`, `ChatsView`). `HomeView` (`Features/Shell/Views`) reads `MyGroupsStore.communities` directly, owns a Mine `TournamentListViewModel` and a `.joined` `EventListViewModel`, runs the three loads concurrently (`async let`) and draws them as `HomeOverview` under `HomeOverviewState` (the pure spinner / empty / sections rule over a `HomeListSnapshot`): a "Your groups" `GroupRowList` above "Your tournaments" and a "Your games" `EventCardList`, one pull-to-refresh over all; creating lives under the "+" on Explore; `HomeContent(for:)` decides whether a guest sees the sign-in prompt instead, and the loads run in one `.task(id:)` keyed by the caller and the events version, so a guest requests nothing and whoever signs in from Home is loaded at once. A launch lands a restored user on Home and everyone else on Explore (`AppNavigation.startTab(for:)`). `ExploreTab` owns the `DiscoverGroupsViewModel` behind `GroupCarousel` (drawn through the shared `TileCarousel`) (two `LazyHGrid` rows of `GroupTile`s, nothing until Discover has answered; it searches once per caller and again after a group changed anywhere, on Explore's pull-to-refresh too, and keeps a failure to the log because the events list reports its own), which `EventListView` takes as the header above its list, with an "Events" heading over the cards while the carousel shows; "See all" pushes `DiscoverGroupsView` (its own Discover view model, `.searchable` in the navigation bar drawer so the field is always shown, `TypeFilterButton` + `TypeFilterPanel`, `DiscoverGroupList` with paging), and Discover works for guests. A Home row calls `AppNavigation.open(group:)` (the detail, on the Home stack) and a Chats row `open(chat:)` (the room, on the Chats stack); a tile or a Discover card is a `NavigationLink(value: group)` to the detail (a join there shows on Home at once, and on the card after its next search). `GroupDetailView` shows the segments `GroupDetailSection.available(for:in:)` allows (Events always; Tournaments while the flag is on and the group is public or the caller is in it; Members per `GroupAccess.canSeeMembers`; the picker only for more than one, falling back to Events when the chosen one disappears), rebuilds its `MembersViewModel` when the caller's access changes (a sign-in on a public group's detail brings the roster), hands every change of the group to `MembersViewModel.update(group:)`, takes roster and edit changes through `GroupDetailViewModel.accept(_:)` (the one place a detail writes Mine), follows Mine through `GroupDetailViewModel.refreshFromStore()` whenever Mine reloaded or a sibling screen changed a group, and confirms leave, delete, remove and ban through one `confirmationDialog(item:)` over `GroupConfirmation`; `isGone` pops the screen. `InvitePeopleSheet` (the detail's Invite button and its menu) mounts its own popup and lists `InvitePeopleViewModel.visible` as `InviteCandidateRow`s under a `lilyField()` search field, with empty states for nobody to invite, no match and a failed load. `ShellSync` (`Features/Shell/Services`, driven by `AppRootView`'s one `.task(id:)` per scene phase and user, so a phase change cancels the run still going for the previous one) runs the per-user sync in order (the account, the realtime connection, Mine, the inbox, the block list, the push offer and registration), stopping at the first await after a cancellation; its `restore()` lands on the start tab and opens a notification tap kept while the session restored, and `AppNavigation.openFromNotification(_:)` is the one push for a tapped game or match (the Chats stack with the chat flag, Home without). `AvatarCircle` draws initials (or a glyph) in tinted glass at `avatarSmall`/`avatarMedium`/`avatarSize`; `String.initials` is the one initials rule (Profile and rosters share it).
- **Groups view models.** One view model per screen under `Features/Groups/ViewModels`, built by the factories in `AppDependencies+Groups.swift`. `DiscoverGroupsViewModel` (Mine has no view model: Home reads `MyGroupsStore.communities`) pages Discover (`query` debounced by `AppConfig.Groups.searchDebounce` over an injected `Sleep` and capped at `AppConfig.Groups.queryMaxLength` UTF-16 units through `String.prefix(wireLength:)`, since Laurel counts `String.length()`, `typeFilter` applied at once, `loadMore()` by cursor against the position the page was searched for, a newer search drops an older answer, a group change or a sign-in landing mid-search leaves the page stale for the next appearance, guests allowed; it records `group_search_performed` with `hasQuery` and `resultCount` only). `GroupDetailViewModel` follows the events detail: join/leave/delete with one `TRY_AGAIN` repeat (`LostRace.attemptTwice`, which the events detail uses too), refusals refetch the group and then show the popup, a `.network`/`.groupActionFailed` refetch that already shows the intent skips it (and `NOT_A_MEMBER` on a leave counts as done); an accepted or joined group goes into Mine through `MyGroupsStore.add`, a leave through `replace`, a delete through `remove`, whose change bump is what Home, the carousel and Discover reload on (no closure back to a list; `EditGroupViewModel` hands its saved group to the detail's `accept`, which writes Mine once per save); `isGone` tells the screen the caller can no longer see the group; `showsOpenChat` is false from the chat (`GroupDetailContext.fromChat`) and while `FeatureFlags.chat` is off. `CreateGroupViewModel` refetches by `draft.clientId` after an unknown outcome and accepts the group when `role == .owner`, through `CreateFlow` (`Core/Networking`), the one send, lookup and ownership path of the event, group and tournament forms (a lookup that finds nothing is the check's answer at debug while the popup shows the create's failure). The plain writes of the members, invite, profile and edit view models and of the tournament detail go through `WriteGate` (`Core/Networking`, built once per view model): one `TRY_AGAIN` repeat via `LostRace.attemptTwice`, which logs the retry line itself, a cancellation kept quiet, the failure logged (warning for a refusal the screen expects, error otherwise) and reported, and a Bool back; the detail view models that refetch-and-settle keep their own catch. `MembersViewModel.actions(for:)` decides the row menu from `GroupAccess` and never offers the caller's own row (a row with no actions gets no menu at all); remove and ban ask first (`MemberAction.isDestructive`, `Action.confirmation(for:)` -> `GroupConfirmation`); a ban reads the group back for its count and epoch, since the backend answers the member alone (a failed read-back is a warning, and the count catches up on the next Mine load); admins also load the banned list. `InvitePeopleViewModel` loads the candidates once per sheet, narrows them with `localizedStandardContains` as the query changes (`content` says whether the sheet shows the spinner, the failed state, "Nobody to invite yet", "Nobody found" or the rows), sends one invite per row at a time (a second tap while one is out is dropped, a `TRY_AGAIN` is repeated once) and flips the row to "Invited" in place; `.alreadyMember` and `.cannotInvite` reach the popup with copy of their own. `InboxViewModel` (`Features/Inbox/ViewModels`) answers an invite from the inbox: Accept adds the group to Mine and opens its chat through `AppNavigation.open(chat:)`, Decline flips the card, a verdict on the invite reloads the inbox after the popup (logged at warning, a product state), an acceptance naming neither group nor tournament (contract drift) warns and reloads Mine, and a reminder that opened its game logs `Reminder <id> opened game <eventId>`; its `isInitialLoad` is "no page answered yet", so the spinner precedes "Nothing here yet". `ReportViewModel` serves a group, a tournament or a message (`ReportTarget`), judging the trimmed comment. Every failure, the chat's included, goes through `GroupErrorReporter`: the shared popup plus `MeStore.noteTermsRequired()` on `.termsRequired`. Log lines under `.groups` and `.inbox` carry ids only, never names or query text.

## Dated notes

- **Groups data layer (2026-09-25, slice S2 of the groups plan; no screens yet).** `Features/Groups/{Models,Interfaces,Implementations,State}`. The group model is `SportGroup`, not `Group`: `Group` is a SwiftUI view and would shadow it in every view file (`AppRootView`, `ProviderButton` use `Group { }`); the `GET /api/me` model is `Account`, because SwiftLint's `type_name` minimum is three characters.
