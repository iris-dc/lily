# Design language, components and the aurora

> The design language as decided with the user, buttons, chips, the aurora and grain, iOS 26 layering, the intro pager and its slides, the app icon. Moved out of `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-17 and kept as written. The reference part describes the feature as the README did; the dated notes record decisions, lessons and the evidence behind them, newest last, so an older sentence can be superseded by a later one or by a marker such as "(removed 2026-10-14)". Read this file before touching the area; the rules that apply everywhere are in `AGENTS.md`.

## Reference (from the README)

- Landing screen: a swipeable intro of four full-page slides, each a plain statement of what the app does (the sports events to find near you, joining one in a tap, groups, tournaments; the groups and tournaments slides show only while their feature flags are on), each a framed miniature of a real screen drawn with the app's own components (the Explore feed over live upcoming events, an event's detail down to a Join with a ring pulsing out of it (`TapPulseCurve`: every frame read off a clock, so growth and fade never drift apart), a group room with its composer, a tournament's bracket with both semi-finals in), vertical page bars at the right edge, and the two ways in ("Explore events" and "Sign in") pinned under every slide, so one tap enters the app from any of them. Sign-in (Apple / Google / Email) is optional and lives in a half-height sheet; the email form (pushed inside it, no grabber over its back button, no field focused on its own so nothing moves until the user taps one) signs in, signs up, and takes the 6-digit confirmation code Cognito emails (with "Resend code" and "Back to sign in"); a confirmed account whose sign-in then fails goes back to the sign-in step with its email and password kept, since the pool refuses a second confirmation.

### App icon

The icon is generated, not drawn by hand: `swift scripts/make-app-icon.swift /tmp/icon` renders a lowercase "i" set in
SF Pro Rounded Black in cream on the dark field (ink black with a maroon drift from the top left), its dot a ball with red
seams, the letter of the name and the ball for the sport, at 1024 pt in the light, dark and tinted appearances iOS asks for
(`final-any/dark/tinted.png`), plus the same icon as two layers for Icon Composer and a strip of the three at home-screen
sizes (`preview-strip.png`). The typeface is read from the Mac at render time; only the PNGs ship. It was chosen on
2026-10-09 from a grid of typefaces and colourways, after two spark icons were rejected. The shipped files live in
`lily/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset` as `AppIcon.png`, `AppIcon-Dark.png` and `AppIcon-Tinted.png`; they are opaque,
as the App Store requires. Judge a change on a simulator's home screen (`xcrun simctl install`, then a screenshot), not in
the asset catalog.

- **Branding lives in `Config/AppBranding.swift`:** app name, tab titles, the two landing buttons (the slides' copy is `AppBranding.Intro` in `AppBranding+Intro.swift`), empty states, sign-in and profile copy, button titles. `AppBranding.Events` holds the capacity copy ("Full", "N spots left" on compact cards, "N of M joined" under the capacity bar; for a game that allows extra players "N more needed" / "N joined" and "N joined · M needed", for one without a limit "N joined", in `AppBranding+Capacity.swift`) and `SportEvent.availabilityText` / `capacityText` are the only formatters, so the map's preview card and the capacity bar cannot disagree. Change copy there and every screen follows; UI tests match on the same literals.
- **Colors** are asset catalog colorsets with light and dark variants, exposed via generated symbols (`Color.lilyAccent`, `Color.lilySecondary`, `Color.lilySurface`, ...). Rust-leaning red `#B72734` is the accent, maroon `#5E0C26` the deep tone behind it, and the aurora core keeps the original raspberry `#B31B3F` in its own colorset (`LilyAuroraCore`) so the accent can be retuned without moving the background; amber (`lilySecondary`) is used sparingly for event-type chips, the hero badge and the "nearly full" capacity state.
- **Buttons are native iOS 26 controls.** `lilyProminentButton(sizing:)` (accent `.glassProminent`) and `lilyGlassButton(sizing:)` (plain `.glass`) in `Core/UI/Components/ButtonModifiers.swift` set the capsule shape, `.controlSize(.large)`, `.buttonSizing(.flexible)` (full width) or `.fitted` (hugging) and the button font; nothing puts a frame on a capsule button's label, so every capsule has the system's 50pt height and padding and grows with Dynamic Type. Text fields use `DesignTokens.Layout.fieldHeight` (50pt) to match the button under them, and text-only (`.borderless`) buttons get a 44pt tappable area through `tappableLabel()`. The `.glass` style colours its label from the tint, so ink labels (landing "Sign in", provider buttons) pass `labelColor: .lilyInk`; "Sign out" keeps the red on purpose. Provider buttons centre glyph and title like the system Sign in with Apple button, and the spinner takes the glyph's place while loading. The landing shows two stacked full-width capsules (prominent "Explore events", glass "Sign in") inside one `GlassEffectContainer`, pinned under the intro pager so every slide offers them.
- **Typography:** SF Pro only, with tight tracking on the wordmark (removed 2026-10-17) and headline (sizes and tracking in `DesignTokens.Typography`).
- **Aurora background** is a 4x4 `MeshGradient` in a 30 fps `TimelineView`, in the spirit of the Raycast home page: a near-black surface with soft luminous red glows and a whisper of grain. Two maroon masses, upper-left behind the headline and lower-right behind the button, are joined by a thin diagonal with a darker valley on either side (`AuroraPalette.roles`), drawn with bicubic smoothing so no cell edge shows. Corners are pinned, edge points slide along their edge, interior points wander on paths that sum three sines drawn from four incommensurate periods (19, 23, 29, 37 s) with golden-angle phase offsets. The red visibly flows because a brightness ripple travels down the main diagonal every 29 s and a weaker one crosses the other diagonal every 41 s (`AuroraGeometry.cellIntensities`), while the raspberry core, kept on the top edge so its peak never swallows the red headline line, breathes every 23 s. The whole background fades in from nothing over 3.5 s (ease-in-out) once per launch, on the wall clock (`AuroraReveal`): every instance reads the same curve, so a tab, a push or a sheet that appears mid-fade continues it instead of restarting from black. Numbers live in `DesignTokens.Aurora`; the math lives in `Core/UI/Effects` (`AuroraGeometry`, `AuroraPalette`, `AuroraReveal`, `FilmGrain`) and is unit tested; `LilyTheme` maps roles to asset colors and the view only draws. Every screen adds live film grain: 12 seeded 128 px noise tiles with a squared distribution (sparse bright speckle, blacks stay black), generated once, drawn at 2 device pixels per noise pixel without interpolation and cycled at 12 fps (a slow shimmer, not a boil), blended `plusLighter` at 5%. Content screens draw the mesh at `contentIntensity` (0.55), the landing at 0.85, the launch screen at full strength (the launch screen removed 2026-10-17). Reduce Motion freezes the first frame and shows it at once. The app is forced to dark mode via the `INFOPLIST_KEY_UIUserInterfaceStyle` build setting; colorsets keep their light variants for when the light theme returns. Phases are reduced modulo their period in `Double` before becoming `Float`: `timeIntervalSinceReferenceDate` cast to `Float` only resolves steps of several radians, and the old mesh stood still for a minute at a time.
- **Liquid Glass layering.** Two rules that iOS 26 enforces and that are easy to get wrong. A toolbar item already draws its own glass capsule, so the segmented list/map `Picker` sets `.sharedBackgroundVisibility(.hidden)` on its `ToolbarItem` to avoid a doubled background. The tab bar floats above content, so `EventsMapView` lets only the `Map` ignore the bottom safe area and keeps the selected-event card as a `ZStack` sibling inside the safe area; a UI test asserts the card stays above the tab bar.
- **Provider glyphs:** Apple and Email are SF Symbols in the ink color (the envelope filled so it weighs the same as the Apple mark); Google uses its official multicolor "G" as a vector asset (`GoogleLogo`). Apple and Google tap through to the popup's "Coming soon" until the pool has identity providers; the Apple button must meet Apple's Sign in with Apple guidelines (or use `SignInWithAppleButton`) once it signs in for real.

- **Asset symbols are generated:** colorsets are `Color.lilyAccent`, `Color.lilySecondary`, `Color.lilySurface`, `Color.lilyInk`, ... and images `Image(.googleLogo)`. Add a colorset, get the symbol. They are compiled into the app module, so they are `@MainActor`-isolated: reference them only from views and other main-actor types, never from a `nonisolated` enum or struct.
- **Design language, decided with the user:** SF Pro only with tight tracking (a serif wordmark was tried and rejected), red accent `#B72734` (`LilyAccent` and `AccentColor`; coral `#FF6363` became dark raspberry `#B31B3F` on 2026-09-09, then was nudged "a tiny bit rustier" to `#B72734` the same day; a brighter `#E8285F` was rejected as too pink) over maroon `#5E0C26` (`LilyAccentDeep`); the aurora core is frozen at the raspberry `#B31B3F` in `LilyAuroraCore` so the background stays put when the accent moves, with amber as a sparingly used secondary, Liquid Glass surfaces, dark only for now (the light theme was removed on 2026-09-09 via `INFOPLIST_KEY_UIUserInterfaceStyle = Dark`; colorsets keep their light variants), native iOS 26 large buttons (50pt; see the Buttons note below). The landing sells the product and enters the app as a guest in one tap; sign-in is optional and lives in a sheet, reached from a full-width glass "Sign in" button centred under the primary one (2026-09-11, replacing the "Already have an account? Sign in" text link at the user's request; a caption-plus-compact-pill and a text-only button were built and judged and lost). `EventTypeChip` and the preview card's icon sit as glass on glass cards on purpose: Apple advises against nesting Liquid Glass, but the render is clean and was approved by eye; revisit only if it ever looks muddy. Removed on 2026-09-09 at the user's request: the Search tab (with the client-side filtering behind it; "I don't need it"), the Explore subtitle "Games near you this week.", and the wordmark on the landing (it stays on the launch screen; both removed 2026-10-17). The reference point for distance is the user's position for now; a chosen place is the intended next step and `EventFilter.maxDistanceMeters` is already judged against an arbitrary origin. Events gained optional details on 2026-09-11 at the user's request: description, "who they are looking for exactly" (free text), skill level and price; all optional, `nil` by default, with `Price` and `SkillLevel` models. The capacity bar's track went faint (same day, user request): `.quaternary` had read as a full grey bar, so an empty game looked full. The label under it now counts joined players the way the bar fills ("N of M joined") instead of "N of M spots left". On Profile the guest section sits directly on the screen at `DesignTokens.Layout.screenMargin` so its heading lines up with the large title; a card there had put the text 24pt further right.
- **iOS 26 layering, both learned from real bugs:** every toolbar item draws its own glass capsule, so a segmented `Picker` inside one needs `.sharedBackgroundVisibility(.hidden)` on the `ToolbarItem`, otherwise it renders two backgrounds and two outlines. The tab bar floats above content, so a full-bleed map must let only the `Map` ignore the bottom safe area, with overlays kept as siblings in a `ZStack`; `.ignoresSafeArea(edges: .bottom)` on the container pins bottom overlays to the screen edge, under the tab bar.
- **Animating with `TimelineView` dates:** `context.date.timeIntervalSinceReferenceDate` is about 8e8 s. A phase like `Float(time / period * 2π)` lands near 1e8-3e8, where one `Float` step is 8-32 rad, so `sin` of it is constant for a minute and then jumps; the aurora shipped static because of this and nobody noticed for days. Reduce modulo the period in `Double` first (`time.truncatingRemainder(dividingBy: period)`), then convert. `AuroraGeometry.angle(at:period:)` does this; reuse it. Byte-identical `simctl` screenshots taken seconds apart are the quick test.
- **Buttons:** every glass or prominent capsule goes through `lilyProminentButton(sizing:)` / `lilyGlassButton(sizing:labelColor:)`, and nothing puts a `frame` on such a label: the old `frame(minHeight: 48)` on the label rendered ~62pt capsules, because the style adds its own padding. Text-only buttons are `.borderless` and get their 44pt hit area from `tappableLabel()`; tappable cards, pins and the popup's dismiss stay `.plain`. Chips share `lilyChip(_:)` for their metrics (`EventTypeChip` badge, the price chip on cards); `EventMapPin` takes its selected look from `LilyTheme.selectionGlass`/`selectionLabelColor` (accent glass + white label when on, plain glass + ink when off) and is a 40pt circle whose selection the `Map` owns. Filter criteria live in the dropdown `EventFilterPanel`; `ChoiceChip` is for multiple choice inside such panels, never for a chip row on a screen (rejected by the user 2026-09-11). `.glass` colours its label from the tint, so a glass button that should read as ink (landing "Sign in", provider buttons) passes `labelColor: .lilyInk` and "Sign out" keeps the accent. `ContentUnavailableView` actions use `.fitted` or they squash into a blob.
- **Every content screen starts with `ContentScreen { ... }`** (aurora at content strength under the content); the landing and launch screens draw `AuroraBackground` themselves at their own intensity (the launch screen removed 2026-10-17). Every instance shows the same frame because mesh, grain **and the fade-in** follow the wall clock: `AuroraReveal.opacity(at:)` eases from 0 to 1 over `revealDuration` from a process-wide `start`, so a tab, push or sheet that appears mid-fade continues it (until 2026-09-28 each instance faded in from black on its own `onAppear`, and every tab switch restarted the background). Do not put the aurora once behind the `TabView` with transparent screens instead: a pushed screen with no opaque background lets the outgoing one show through during the slide. `AppRootView` crossfades on every root switch (`rootScreen`: launch, landing, shell), so launch -> landing is as soft as landing -> shell now that the background underneath is continuous. Under Reduce Motion the frozen frame is shown at once: the paused timeline cannot drive the fade.
- **Sheets need their own popup mount.** `.errorPopup` on the root view renders under any presented sheet, so a sheet that can report errors (the sign-in sheet) mounts `.errorPopup(errorCenter)` itself and takes the `ErrorCenter` explicitly; `ErrorCenter` tracks the mounts and only the topmost one draws, so the error is not shown twice.
